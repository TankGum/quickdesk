Questions?

      

        

          Which Linux distributions are supported?
          
Any x86_64 distro from the last few years: Ubuntu 22.04 and newer, Debian 12, Fedora, Linux Mint and the like. QuickDesk works best on GNOME, where it registers its shortcuts for you. On other desktops, bind quickdesk toggle notes, clipboard or ports to a key in your desktop’s keyboard settings.

        

        

          Do the shortcuts work on Wayland?
          
Yes. QuickDesk adds them to GNOME’s custom keyboard shortcuts, so they work in both X11 and Wayland sessions, and removes them again when it quits.

        

        

          Why Super + Alt and not Super + Shift?
          
Vietnamese input methods such as Unikey and Bamboo swallow Super + Shift + letter while you type in a text field; combinations with Alt keep working. You can rebind any shortcut in Settings → Hotkeys, and QuickDesk tells you if one is already taken.

        

        

          How do notes sync between machines?
          
There is no QuickDesk account or server. You connect each machine to the same S3-compatible bucket you own — Cloudflare R2’s free tier is plenty — and enter the same passphrase. The first machine also gives you a recovery key. After that notes sync in the background, offline edits catch up when you reconnect, and if a note was edited on two machines at once, the other version is kept as a copy.

        

        

          Is my data encrypted?
          
Notes are encrypted on your device (XChaCha20-Poly1305, with a key derived from your passphrase using Argon2id) before upload, so the storage provider only ever sees ciphertext. The bucket’s secret key is kept in your system keyring. Clipboard history and port data never leave your machine.

        

        

          Is my clipboard history uploaded anywhere?
          
No. It stays on this device and never syncs. Copies that password managers mark as secret are skipped, and you can pause saving for 15 minutes, an hour or until you turn it back on. QuickDesk keeps the latest 1,000 entries (pinned ones are kept forever) and images up to 10 MB each. Clear it anytime.

        

        

          How does auto-paste work?
          
When you pick an entry, QuickDesk switches back to the app you were using and presses Shift + Insert, which also works in terminals. By default it does this through GNOME’s remote-desktop portal, which asks for permission once. You can instead enable instant paste through a virtual keyboard in Settings → Auto-paste (asks for your administrator password once), or turn auto-paste off and just copy.

        

        

          What does the AI usage ring read?
          
For Claude Code, QuickDesk reads the login Claude Code already saved on your machine and asks Anthropic for your usage, at most every 5 minutes. The token is only read — never stored, refreshed or sent anywhere except Anthropic. Codex and Antigravity numbers come from the log files those tools keep on your computer.

        

        

          Is QuickDesk free?
          
Yes. QuickDesk is free and open source under the MIT license. The source code is on GitHub.

        

        

          How do I uninstall it?
          
Turn off “Start QuickDesk when I log in” in Settings, quit from the tray, then run sudo apt remove quick-desk (Debian/Ubuntu) or sudo dnf remove QuickDesk (Fedora), or just delete the AppImage. Your notes and clipboard history stay in ~/.local/share/io.github.tankgum.quickdesk until you delete that folder.
